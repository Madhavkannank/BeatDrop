Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

1. Data Collection & Processing

BeatDrop is an open Big Data music lakehouse analytics demonstrator. We process publicly available song metadata including track names, artists, popularity scores, genres, and release years across distributed lake layers (Raw, Processed, Curated, Analytics, Quarantine).

2. Groq AI Enrichment

When you query song metadata via Groq AI, your search term is transmitted securely to the Groq API to extract canonical track schema attributes. Personal user credentials or browser fingerprints are never stored or monetized.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

BeatDrop uses privacy-friendly browser local storage exclusively for your cookie consent preference and client-side sorting preferences. No cross-site advertising trackers are deployed.

4. Contact & Open Source Inquiries

For questions or architecture verification, inspect the repository documentation or project license.
